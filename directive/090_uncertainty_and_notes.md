# SAMA Uncertainty and Notes

**Status:** Draft
**Purpose:** Record material uncertainty and analysis limitations without changing the JSON schema.

## Notes
- Use the existing top-level `notes` array.
- Do not add a separate uncertainty field or alter the schema.
- State what is uncertain, why the evidence is insufficient, and which conclusions or relationships are omitted or qualified.
- Record missing references, unresolved dynamic targets, ambiguous dispatch, incomplete source coverage, and distinctions between static evidence and unverified runtime behavior.
- Keep notes concise, specific, and useful.
- Do not use notes to introduce unsupported entities or relationships.
